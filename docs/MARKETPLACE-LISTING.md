# BizPro - Professional Multi-Purpose Business Template

## 🎯 Template Overview

BizPro is a modern, fully responsive business website template designed for agencies, startups, corporate websites, and professional service providers. Built with clean HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, this template offers exceptional performance, easy customization, and a professional design that will make your business stand out.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Download and extract the template
2. Open `index.html` in your browser
3. Customize colors in `assets/css/main.css`
4. Replace placeholder content with your own
5. Add your images to `assets/images/`
6. Deploy to your hosting (see deployment guide)
