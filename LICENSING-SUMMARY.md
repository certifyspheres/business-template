## 🚀 Quick Decision Guide

### Choose Open Source (FREE) if:
- ✅ You can show attribution on your website
- ✅ Personal or hobby project
- ✅ Open source project
- ✅ Non-profit organization
- ✅ Educational project
- ✅ Budget is limited

### Choose Commercial (PAID) if:
- ✅ Cannot or don't want attribution
- ✅ Business/corporate website
- ✅ Client projects (agency/freelancer)
- ✅ SaaS or web application
- ✅ Need priority support
- ✅ Want to resell or white-label
